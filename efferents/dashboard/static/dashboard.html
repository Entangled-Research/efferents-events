Use your saved recovery key, or a still-valid network token or owner link. This restores your identity and labs in any browser, including incognito.

Keep your identity

Save your recovery key in a password manager before leaving. It works after cookies are cleared or your 48-hour token expires, and renews access to the same labs and budget. Anyone with this key can act as you. It stays valid until replaced.

2 · Give it your network token when it asks

The token is you. It pays for your lab's research model calls from the event budget and marks the lab as yours on the network. Never share it.

3 · Sharpen and steer from that folder

The harness sharpens your claim until it can fail, builds the lab, and starts it. Your lab appears on the network within a minute. Open your lab to steer, pause or resume it in the browser, or use these commands from its folder: 

efferents steer --submission . "Prioritise the small buckets."
efferents steer --submission . --pause     # or --resume
efferents stop  --submission .

Use your owner link while your token is valid. Save a recovery key above for later access to the same labs and budget.

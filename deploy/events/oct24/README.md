# October 24 starter package

Three tracks, seven independent idea contracts, CPU executors and scoped measurement plans. The versioned manifest is the source for the participant guide and organizer setup. Copy the tracks into the October event volume's `cluster/tracks/` and restart hub/sync to load them. Keep provider and owner credentials outside this directory and outside Git.

Design iterations:

1. Scope: replace broad protein folding and large image training with measured GFP fluorescence and small controlled samplers so the first comparison can run on a laptop.
2. Scientific review: keep all 64 pixels in both encoding arms and disclose 6-versus-64-qubit resource differences; preserve complex phase and count actual tensor-core storage; separate GFP random and mutational-distance holdouts; keep held-out labels out of fits; preserve failures.
3. Smoke review: shape validity saturates on the analytic denoiser, so the few-step contract also requires diversity retention. Schedules are compared at the same number of denoiser calls. The spatial baseline sees RGB pixels, never generator coordinates. Thresholds remain provisional event preregistrations; smoke checks do not establish a finding.
4. Onboarding: use automatic model-led refinement and an internal contract check; no external probe skill, checkout or repetitive approval dialogue. Owner plans can be edited in the live console, with version history, then applied by the coding agent before local research starts.

The image fixture is deliberately an analytic finite-mixture diffusion model, not a pretrained neural network or VLM benchmark. The quantum fixture is simulated and does not demonstrate entanglement or advantage. GFP data are attributed and checksum-pinned under the original CC BY 4.0 license. Full neural samplers and VLM reasoning are optional extensions after checking compute and evaluation compatibility.

Prepared organizer labs are stopped, with zero research measurements and no published results. CPU executor smoke checks are validation, not campaign evidence. Local source folders and private account credentials are held separately; do not copy them into the public package.

5. Quantum revision: replaced the measurement/calibration ideas with amplitude-versus-RY encoding on UCI digits and complex Fourier image compression with TT-SVD. Fixed train/test splits and ridge readout; no hardware advantage claim. Bond-2 storage is 40 complex coefficients versus 64 dense; thresholds remain unchanged even when a smoke result misses them.

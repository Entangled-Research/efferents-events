## Lab 2 — Uncertainty and Hallucination

“Maybe transformers hallucinate more when they’re less certain about the answer,
and we can use this to flag potential hallucination.”

This is an example of one starting hypothesis splitting into two related
research hypotheses that work together:

1. “Maybe transformers hallucinate more when they’re less certain about the answer.”
2. “Maybe we can use that uncertainty to flag potential hallucination.”

The first investigates the relationship between uncertainty and hallucination.
The second investigates whether that relationship is useful for flagging answers
that may be hallucinated. Evidence from the first informs the second; a
relationship alone does not establish a useful detector.
